      Pipeline

      1. Data science: 145k daily observations from the Kaggle weatherAUS dataset; median imputation; engineered humidity and pressure deltas, temperature range and wind direction as sin/cos.
      2. Machine learning: logistic regression, random forest, gradient boosting and an MLP neural network, compared on a chronological split (train to 2014, validate 2015, test 2016–17).
      3. Soft computing: a Mamdani fuzzy system with 18 expert rules, a fuzzy IMD rain-type classifier, and a genetic algorithm that evolves the blend weights and decision threshold for best F1.
      4. Live and historical data: Open-Meteo current conditions, past 7 days and 10-year climatology, which acts as a Bayesian prior.
      5. IoT: ESP32 sensor readings go to the same engine; any missing values are imputed.
